Store relocation

Relocating a store is much more than moving furniture. We carefully dismantle furniture, displays, lighting and graphics so that every piece arrives in perfect condition.

We pack and palletise everything with tailored protection, inventory and label it, and organise shipping to any destination, in Spain or abroad.

At the new location we reinstall the store following the drawings and brand guidelines, remove packaging and waste and leave the premises clean and ready to trade.

What’s included

  • Dismantling of furniture, displays and lighting
  • Packing and palletising with protection for every piece
  • Inventory and labelling for an error-free installation
  • Transport and shipping to any destination
  • Complete installation at the new location
  • Waste removal and final clean
